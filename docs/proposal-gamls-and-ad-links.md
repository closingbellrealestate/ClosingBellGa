## Listing Ad Links

Every listing gets a **"Generate Ad Link"** button. One click creates a ready-to-use link for Facebook/Instagram ads that opens a clean, mobile-friendly landing page for that home. Anyone who fills out the short form becomes a lead in your system — tagged so you know exactly which listing and ad it came from.

**What you get:**
- A "Generate Ad Link" button on every listing.
- Polished landing pages branded for ClosingBell, built to convert paid-ad traffic.
- Leads captured and tagged by listing/campaign so you can see what's working.
- Works correctly inside the Facebook and Instagram in-app browsers.
- Includes **3 starter landing-page designs** to cover common campaigns.
